About Aeron

Your Industrial Gas Partner in the UAE

Aeron Industrial Gases LLC supplies industrial gases to businesses across the UAE. From automotive plants to pharmaceutical facilities, we deliver the quality and reliability your operations demand.

Who We Are

Serving UAE Industries

Aeron Industrial Gases LLC was established to serve the UAE market with reliable, high-purity industrial gases. We work with manufacturers, pharmaceutical companies, food processors, and other industries that need consistent access to quality gases.

Our parent company, Manav Gases Pvt. Ltd., brings 25+ years of manufacturing expertise. With 12 production facilities producing 600 MT/day of CO₂ and 100 MT/day of Dry Ice, we have the scale to handle everything from small cylinder orders to bulk industrial supply.

ISO 9001:2015 certified, FSSAI approved, with a fleet of specialized tankers — we deliver quality you can count on.

Our Capabilities

Built on Proven Expertise

Aeron brings decades of industrial gas manufacturing experience to the UAE market.

1997

Our manufacturing foundation began with ammonia production, establishing quality standards that continue today.

2006

Expanded into Liquid Carbon Dioxide production, adding critical capabilities for food and beverage industries.

2009

Launched Dry Ice manufacturing in multiple formats — nuggets, pellets, and blocks — for cold chain and industrial cleaning.

2018

Achieved full vertical integration with direct CO₂ manufacturing, ensuring consistent quality and supply reliability.

2023

Scaled to 600 MT/day LCO₂ and 100 MT/day Dry Ice capacity. Added Argon and Oxygen production across multiple facilities.
