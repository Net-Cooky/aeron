Compliance & Safety

All manufacturing facilities comply with Industrial Safety & Health Hazards regulations. Periodic safety audits are conducted per the Factories Act, and all ammonia storage, cylinder filling, and Liquid CO₂ operations are PESO-certified.

Bulk Anhydrous Ammonia and Liquid CO₂ are transported in specialized, compliant tankers. Every delivery to UAE customers meets international safety standards.
